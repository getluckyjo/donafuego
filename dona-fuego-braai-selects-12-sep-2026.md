## Notes on the set

- **The cameras did different jobs.** The disposables gave the occasion — grain, leaks,
  half-cut heads, the toast. Bernard's phone gave a product shoot good enough for trade.
  The Charmera sits between the two and quietly filled the biggest gap in the brief
  (meat on the grid). The underwater camera produced the strangest and most distinctive
  frames of the lot.
- **Variety in the A set:** fourteen different people, across afternoon, dusk, dinner,
  sunset and underwater.
- **Volume warning:** 332 frames in B is more grid than a year of posting. The B bucket
  needs a second pass against a calendar, not more selecting in the abstract.

### For the next shoot

- Assign owners for the end-of-day frames. Nobody stayed for the empties, on any camera.
- Clear competitor bottles from the area before cameras go out, and park cars out of
  sight of the terrace.
- Two underwater cameras, not one.
- The Charmera earned its place. More of those, fewer phones.
