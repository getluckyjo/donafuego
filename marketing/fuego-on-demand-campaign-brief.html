The camcorder

One 90s camcorder, handed around all afternoon into dusk. Nobody's assigned to it — whoever picks it up keeps it rolling on whatever feels like a moment. This is the footage the nostalgic hero edit and the loosest Story cutdowns come from.

What film can't be trusted with

Fixed-focus lenses and a built-in flash can't read a phone screen or a delivery bag logo. Pull one of the eight aside for thirty seconds, twice, on someone's actual phone: the Sixty60 order confirmation, and the delivery hand-off at the gate. Everything else is fair game for the disposables.

216 Frames shot (8 × 27 exp)
~85 Usable stills, in focus & exposed
~40 Feed-ready hero stills
4–5 hrs Raw camcorder footage

Disposable cameras run roughly a 40% usable rate in untrained hands — light leaks, thumbs, half the roll shot at the wrong distance for the flash. That's normal, not a failure; it's what real film from a real party looks like. The camcorder hours cut down to roughly 10–15 short Reels/Story moments plus one 60–90 second nostalgic hero edit.

Influencer gifting

Who gets the box

R1,500/month covers product and courier for 3–5 creators — a gifting relationship, not a paid-post fee. At this budget, fit and engagement rate matter more than follower count.

Fit criteria
  • Geography — Cape Town or Joburg metro, inside Sixty60 coverage. A great creator with a Durban audience is off-target here.
  • Size — 8,000–60,000 followers. Nano to micro, gifting-viable.
  • Content — braai, home entertaining, cocktails/mixology. Not a general lifestyle account posting one drink.
  • Engagement — ≥3% on recent posts, weighted above reach.
Before sending product
  • Check engagement rate across the last 10 posts, not a single viral outlier.
  • Audience location split confirms a real Sixty60-zone following.
  • No conflicting or exclusive alcohol brand partnerships.
  • Comments read as real conversation, not bot replies.
What gets sent

One mixed 4-pack — margarita and paloma — with a one-line brief: "show us your cooler box moment." No script, natural mention only, plus usage rights to repost into the UGC slot. Same 18+/drink-responsibly standard as the paid creative applies to whatever they post.

The reorder multiplier
