The drink you bring. Never the drink you need.

We post the braai, the stoep and the long Saturday lunch. Fuego is in the cooler box because good people are already having a good time. Who's bringing the Fuego?

A fix is a dose of something you can't do without. It sells a craving, and the code bars alcohol as a remedy and anything near drug culture.

Do

  • Lead with people, food and place. Hands, tongs, smoke, a sea view. The can is a guest at the braai, never the reason.

  • Sell what's in the can. Real tequila, real lemon and lime, real grapefruit, ice. Taste is always safe ground.

  • Use bring-and-share words. Bring, share, pour, crack, clink, toast, a round, the cooler box.

  • Let the setting do the relaxing. Sun, shade and a late lunch make the afternoon easy. Never the tequila.

  • Joke about the occasion. Braai-tong politics, who forgot the ice. Never about drinking.

  • Give the Non-Alc Margarita an equal seat. Same table, same adults, same rules. It carries the Fuego name, so it is never the "driver's drink".

  • Tag every post. "Not for Persons Under the Age of 18" on every image, story frame and reel, and at the end of every caption. Account age limit: 18+.

  • Moderate daily. Remove non-compliant comments and tags within 24 hours. Paid creators must look 25+ and use #ad wherever they post.

Don't

  • No fix, need, cure, dose, therapy, self-care or reward. Fuego is enjoyed, never needed. "One for emotional support" was banned in the UK in 2026.

  • No mood makeovers. Stressed, crack a can, happy. The ARB pulled exactly that from Strongbow in 2025.

  • No escaping the week. "Survive Monday", "wine o'clock", "5 o'clock somewhere", Friday countdowns.

  • The can is never why it works. No confidence, wingman, romance, wealth or status. Don Billiato's "taste of wealth" was pulled in 2024.

  • No strength. No "7%", "stronger" or "bar-strength" in posts or graphics. Alcohol content stays on the label.

  • No volume or speed. "How many cans deep?", empties as trophies, downing, drinking games, "one won't hurt".

  • Keep cans off the beach and out of the water. No sand, towels, water's edge, pool, boats, cars or sport. A closed Brutal Fruit bottle on a beach towel lost at the ARB in 2025.

  • No one young, drunk, pregnant or pressured. No teasing the friend who isn't drinking, no other alcohol brands, nothing sexual, nothing a child would love.

How the big brands do it

Heineken · Worlds Apart, 2017. Strangers who disagree build a bar together, then talk it out over a beer. The conversation is the story. UK sales rose 7.3% in 12 weeks.

Guinness · A Lovely Day, 2025. "When you're with your people, it's always a lovely day." The people make the day; Guinness 0.0 sits beside the stout.

Castle Lager · Heritage Day, 2025. The World's Biggest Braai, launched from a billboard grill on Vilakazi Street. The braai and the country are the heroes.

Patrón, 2026. "Made with just three ingredients and nothing else." It sells what's in the bottle, like our label.

Diageo's own code lets a brand be "the perfect choice for already energetic or relaxing occasions", never "the catalyst for any change" in mood. The word that does the work is already.

Before you postSix checks

  • The good time started before the can arrived.
  • Under-18 line on the image and in the caption.
  • No strength, no volume, no mood change.
  • Everyone with a can is an adult who looks 25+.
  • No sand, water, cars or anyone who looks drunk.
  • Giveaways: written rules, 18+ only, free to enter.
