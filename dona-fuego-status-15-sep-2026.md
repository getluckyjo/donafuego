## The hero set is 72, not 20

The first 20-frame set was rejected: too styled, too much like a brand shoot. It was
rebuilt around **soul over can** — a frame with no can but real feeling beats a clean
product shot — and reopened to the B bucket, the underwater roll and Johannes's own 29
green-tagged picks. The four posed product frames were pulled into **TRADE — CLEAN
CAN**, which is where anything Checkers-facing now lives. The A folder is flat and the
shotlist no longer governs it.

## The grade

One pass across all 679 frames so five cameras read as one afternoon: each source's
channel means pulled toward the mean of the four warm film rolls, damped per source,
highlight knee at 0.80, shadows lifted, grain and halation added to the phone frames.
Warmth spread went from 44.8 to 20.5, excluding the underwater roll, which was left
alone on purpose.
