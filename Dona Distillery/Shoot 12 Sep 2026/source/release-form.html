Today's braai is a content shoot for Doña Fuego. Photographs, video and sound recorded here may be used to market the brand, and the pictures you take on the camera we hand you are part of that. This page is you saying that's fine. It takes thirty seconds, and there's a line at the bottom for anything you'd rather not be in — that's an easy yes, so use it.
