# Social specs

## Sizes

| Placement | Pixels | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram feed — **default** | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 |
| Instagram feed — square | 1080 × 1080 | 1:1 |
| Stories / Reels | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 |
| Carousel | 1080 × 1350, consistent across all slides | 4:5 |
| Profile picture | 320 × 320 min | 1:1 |

**Default to 4:5.** It takes the most vertical space in the feed. Only go
square when the image genuinely needs the width.

Export JPEG, quality 85–90, sRGB. Under 1MB per image.

## Safe areas

- **Stories:** keep text and faces out of the top 250px and bottom 320px.
  That's where the profile bar and the reply box land.
- **Feed:** don't put anything critical in the outer 60px. Grid crops
  4:5 to square in the profile view.

## The grid

The profile grid is a composition, not a queue. Before scheduling, lay
the next nine out together and check:

- **No two adjacent posts look the same.** Alternate close-up and wide,
  people and product, warm and cool.
- **Product appears roughly every third post.** More often and it's a
  catalogue. Less and nobody knows what we sell.
- **Faces in at least a third of them.** Faces are what make a brand feel
  like people.

## Post types in rotation

| Type | Roughly | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Occasion | 40% | The braai, the cooler box, the Saturday. Builds the world |
| Product | 25% | The can, in a hand, in ice, in context |
| People | 20% | Guests, the founder, the distillery, real customers |
| Utility | 15% | Where to buy, what's in it, a recipe, a stockist |

## Captions

See `02-voice.md`. Short. The first line is the only one most people
read — put the whole idea there, not a wind-up.

## Hashtags

Few and real. Three to five, in the first comment. A wall of thirty
reads as desperate and doesn't work anyway. Our own tag goes on
everything so we can find the content people post back.

## Reposting customer content

1. Ask before you post, every time, in writing
2. Credit the account in the caption, not just the tag
3. Check the person's other content before you amplify them
4. Run it past `07-compliance.md` — their photo, our liability

## Before scheduling anything

Check `07-compliance.md`. Paid boosting has stricter rules than organic
and the penalties land on us, not on the platform.
