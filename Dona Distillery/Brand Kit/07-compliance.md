# Compliance — read before every post

South Africa regulates alcohol marketing tightly. This is a **working
checklist, not legal advice.** Get the current ARA Code of Commercial
Communication and have an attorney confirm anything you're unsure of,
especially before a paid campaign.

The cost of getting this wrong isn't one deleted post — it's the account,
the relationship with Checkers, and the brand's credibility.

## Never, in any image or video

- **Anyone under 18.** And nobody who could *read* as under 25, even if
  they're older. If there's any doubt, don't use the frame.
- **Anyone appearing drunk**, unsteady, or asleep. Enjoying it is fine.
  Visibly drunk is not.
- **Any driving context.** Car keys, a car interior, a boat being driven,
  a bicycle. Not even as a joke.
- **Another alcohol brand.** A competitor can, a wine label, branded
  glassware.
- Anything sexual, degrading, or that links the drink to sexual success
- Anything that mocks people who don't drink
- Sport, driving, swimming, or any activity where alcohol raises risk

## Never, in any caption or claim

- Health, medical, nutritional or wellness claims of any kind
- Any suggestion the drink brings success, confidence, popularity or
  attractiveness
- Anything about the strength as a selling point — no "hits hard",
  no "get wrecked", no drinking-game framing
- Anything appealing to under-18s: cartoons, toys, children's
  characters, school references, teen slang

## Age-gating — non-negotiable

- **Set the Instagram and Facebook account minimum-age restriction** to
  18+ (South Africa's legal drinking age). Do this on the account
  settings, not per post.
- **Every boosted post and paid ad must target 18+ only.** Check this on
  each campaign, not once.
- Any website, competition or sign-up form we run needs an age gate.

## Competitions and giveaways

- 18+ only, stated in the rules
- Never make buying more the route to winning more
- Full terms and conditions published and linked
- Consumer Protection Act rules apply — have the terms checked

## Repost and user-generated content

Someone else's photo becomes our liability the moment we share it.
Run it through this full list first. Get written permission. And if
you can't verify the age of everyone in the frame, don't post it.

## Responsible drinking

Carry the standard responsible-drinking message where required. Don't
treat it as a sticker — if it's on the post, it should be legible.

## Before anything goes live

- [ ] Nobody under 18, and nobody reading under 25, in frame with a can
- [ ] Nobody appears intoxicated
- [ ] No driving, vehicles or risk activity
- [ ] No other alcohol brand visible
- [ ] No health, strength, success or sexual claims
- [ ] Account age restriction is on
- [ ] If boosting: 18+ targeting confirmed on this campaign
- [ ] If it's someone else's image: written permission on file
