# The brand

## What it is

Doña Fuego is a tequila RTD from Doña Distillery — a 100% female-owned
distillery in Cape Town, founded 2019 by Danielle Schoeman.

Four cans, 250ml, 7% ABV:

- **Margarita** — green
- **Spicy Margarita** — red
- **Paloma** — pink
- **Non-Alcoholic Margarita**

Classified **Spirit Cooler** on-pack. The label line is:
*"Made with real tequila, lemon & lime juice. No BS."*

Retail pricing and store counts change — check the current numbers with
Danielle before putting either in a caption or an ad.

## The one-sentence version

Real tequila and real juice in a can, built for the cooler box.

## The occasion we own

**Share of cooler box.** We are not competing for the nightclub or the
cocktail bar. We are competing for two cans in the cooler box on a
Saturday — against Brutal Fruit and Bernini, not against a bottle of
tequila.

The problem we solve: you want a margarita at a braai, on a boat, at the
beach. You do not want to carry a bottle, a mixer, limes, salt and a
shaker to get one.

Every piece of content should be placeable in that moment. If you can't
picture it in a cooler box, on a stoep, or in someone's hand outdoors,
it's off-brief.

## Who we're talking to

Cosmopolitan, social, trend-aware, and **already drinks tequila.** We are
not spending a cent converting people who don't. The working persona is
"Sunny from Sea Point" — she has an opinion about where she drinks,
she's the one who brings something to the braai, and she is instantly
suspicious of anything that looks like an advert.

The goal is not reach. The goal is a founding base of repeat customers
who post about it themselves.

## Why we can charge more

Real imported tequila, real juice, and a higher ABV than the mainstream
alternatives. That's the whole argument and it should be made by showing
the can and the occasion, not by claiming "premium".

## The feeling

Vintage cantina. Sun-bleached, hand-drawn, warm, a bit worn in. Mexico
by way of the Atlantic seaboard. Made by people, not by a marketing
department.

## Three tests for any piece of content

1. **Could this be any other drink?** If yes, it's not ours yet.
2. **Would Sunny screenshot it or scroll past it?**
3. **Does it look like a friend made it, or like an agency made it?**
