# Photography

## The look

Real, warm, slightly imperfect, always outdoors or in a real room.
The reference is a good afternoon someone happened to photograph — not a
product shoot.

Our house style leans on **disposable film and point-and-shoot digital**,
shot by the people actually at the occasion. Grain, flash, light leaks,
off-centre framing and motion blur are features, not faults. They're
what makes it read as true.

## What we shoot

- The cooler box, opened, from above — ice, cans, hands
- A can being cracked
- The pour, over ice, mid-stream
- A cold can sweating in the sun, label facing camera
- Fire, tongs, smoke, meat on the grid
- The table — salads, breads, cans, all of it
- Hands. Always hands
- Flash portraits, eyes open
- People laughing who don't know they're being photographed
- The end of it — empties, stacked plates, melted ice

## What we don't shoot

- Studio product on a seamless background
- Anything with a model looking down the barrel and smiling on cue
- Stock-looking "friends toasting" setups
- Bottles, cocktail shakers, bar tools — we're the opposite of that
- Pristine, styled-to-death surfaces with nothing human in them

## Grading the scans

Keep the set consistent so a grid reads as one afternoon:

- **Warm the mids.** The brand lives in cream, terracotta and coral.
  Cool, blue-grey images fall out of the palette immediately.
- **Lift the blacks slightly.** Film doesn't have true black and neither
  should these.
- **Don't over-sharpen.** Leave the softness; it's the point.
- **Keep the grain.** Never run noise reduction on a film scan.
- **Keep light leaks and flash hot-spots.** They are the character.
- **Don't clone out the mess.** Empties, crumbs, a wet tea towel — all of
  that is what makes it real.

If an image needs heavy retouching to work, it isn't a select. Pick a
different frame.

## Product frames

The exception. Any image going to Checkers point-of-sale or the press
pack needs a **clean, legible can**:

- No dents, no scuffs, no fingerprints on the label
- Nothing covering the word FUEGO
- Condensation is good. Smears are not
- Shot from a normal distance, not so close the can distorts

## Credit

Images shot by guests get credited to whoever shot them when we post
them. Keep a record of who shot what when the scans come back.
