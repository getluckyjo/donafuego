Rules
  • Cream is the default. A white page stops looking like us immediately.
  • Chilli is a seasoning. One eyebrow, one hero word, one button. Three red things fighting means it's gone wrong.
  • Never pure black or cool grey. Ink is warm on purpose.
  • Lime only on dark. Unreadable on cream.

Work Sans — all running text

Real tequila and real juice in a can, built for the cooler box. Two cans on a Saturday, no bottle, no shaker, no mixing.

400 for body, 600 for emphasis, italic for asides. Nothing heavier. Cap the lede at 46 characters per line.

Words we use

tequila · real juice · cooler box · braai · ice · cold · Saturday · grab-and-go · no mixing · the good stuff

Words we don't

Three tests
  • Could this be any other drink?
  • Would she screenshot it, or scroll past?
  • Does it look like a friend made it, or an agency?

Compliance before every post. Nobody under 25 in frame with a can, nobody drunk, no vehicles, no other brands, 18+ on every boost.
