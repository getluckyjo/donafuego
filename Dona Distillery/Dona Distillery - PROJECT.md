## Strategy Agreed (Session 1, June 2026)

1. **Product-market fit is the marketing question.** No broad strategy — define the audience tightly, have a real conversation with them.
2. **Lead with existing tequila drinkers.** Don't spend money converting anyone.
3. **"Share of cooler box"** framing — compete for two cans in the cooler box vs Brutal Fruit / Bernini. Core problem solved: grab-and-go margaritas for holidays/braais (no expensive bottle + mixing kit).
4. **Target: 1,000 repeat customers in the first 6 months** (~2 four-packs/month each), realistically findable within 100km of the distillery. Focus beats spread. → *The 100km radius is superseded by first actuals; see Revised Persona below. The 1,000-customer target and the focus-beats-spread principle stand.*
5. **Persona work:** "Sunny from Sea Point" as starting persona — cosmopolitan, social, trend-aware. Map 10 personas × 10 occasions; pick 1–2 occasions to own. → *Sunny is superseded by first actuals; see Revised Persona below. The 10×10 exercise still to run, now against store data rather than intuition.*
6. **Launch campaign — Field Bar partnership:** buy ~50 Field Bars; they email their database announcing Dona Fuego in Checkers; in-store giveaways at top stores; #DonaFuego photo competition; use entries to learn where/how people drink it.
7. **Duchess/Yuppiechef playbook:** go deep with a few partners rather than broad; double down on what works.
8. **"1,000 Top Fans" living file:** AI-maintained audience file scanning mentions/photos to inform future campaigns.
9. **Capital raise — timing window:** brand structure plan is to build inside the distillery, then move to a separate entity and sell equity (her standard model). Raise on the blue-sky story + Checkers listing now (~R20m brand valuation possible) *before* actuals anchor the number lower. Build the pitch deck and data room now. Prefer a **strategic partner** over straight equity.

## Revised Persona — First Actuals (Aug 2026)

Two months of Checkers store-level data plus Instagram engagement invert the founding persona on all three axes. Source: Danielle's read of the store-level report, 31 Aug 2026.

| | "Sunny from Sea Point" (Jun 2026) | What the tills say (Aug 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| **Geography** | Sea Point; Cape wine farms on weekends | Gauteng north — Centurion, Fourways, Rivonia, Kyalami, Ferndale, Waterfall Corner, Leaping Frog, Irene Village, Southgate |
| **Consumer** | Cosmopolitan, trend-aware, founder's own age and circle | Black women, roughly 30–40 |
| **Occasion** | Weekend wine farm | Unmapped — to be established by the 10×10 exercise. Note the aspiration is Cape/international even where the buyer is not |

**The evidence:**

- **Top stores are Gauteng, overwhelmingly.** Centurion and the northern Joburg corridor carry a large majority of sell-through.
- **Bottom-ten stores are Western Cape coastal and winelands** — Franschhoek, Hout Bay, Hermanus, Strand, and one further coastal store (name to confirm from the report). Effectively zero sales. This is Sunny's own catchment: the persona's neighbourhood is the worst-selling geography in the country.
- **Instagram engagement runs Black, female, ~30–40** — the people posting and commenting on the Checkers ads are consistently that profile, independent of the till data and pointing the same way.

**Consequences:**

1. **The 100km-from-distillery radius (Strategy 4) is dead.** It was chosen for logistical convenience, not evidence. The customers are in Gauteng, ~1,400km from the distillery. The 1,000-customer target itself is unaffected — only where to go looking for them.
2. **Sunny (Strategy 5) is retired as the lead persona.** Not demoted to secondary: the store data puts her in the bottom decile. Any residual Cape Town play needs its own justification.
3. **The planned social shoot is casting the old persona.** It is built around the founder's own friends — her age, her city, her circle. That is Sunny rendered as a cast list. See Shoot Direction below.
4. **Occasion is now the open question, not audience.** We know who is buying. We do not yet know what for. That is what the 10×10 exercise has to answer, and it now has real data to run against.

**Why the Western Cape sells nothing — the aspiration reading.** Doña Fuego is an aspirational buy: badge value, identity signalling, a brand people use to build their own. The Cape and international look is what carries that signal. Someone already living in Franschhoek or Hout Bay does not need the badge — she is living the thing the brand signals. The Cape zeros are therefore not a demand failure but the mechanism working as designed, and they are no argument for changing the visual world of the brand.

This reframes the persona split cleanly: **Cape as setting, Gauteng as audience.** The imagery stays aspirational and Cape/international; the casting, the media spend and the creator partnerships all point at Gauteng north.

**Secondary caveat, still worth closing:** some of the Western Cape zeros may also be a distribution artefact — small-format stores, poor facings, or no stock. Worth confirming against the report, though it no longer changes the creative direction either way.

## Shoot Direction (Aug 2026)

Danielle raised whether to add a *secondary* shoot to capture the demographic actually buying. Recommendation: **no secondary shoot — recast the primary.** A secondary shoot puts the real customer in a supplementary folder, keeps the old persona as hero, and costs a second budget to reach where one recast shoot lands directly.

- **Location carries as much signal as casting.** A Cape wine-farm setting reads as Sunny regardless of who is in frame. Shoot in Gauteng, or shoot location-neutral.
- **Cast the commenters, not the friends.** The women already engaging on the Checkers ads are the "1,000 Top Fans" file (Action 3). Casting from them solves casting, seeds the fan system, secures content rights, and converts cast into advocates in one move.
- **Nails as a hero product shot.** Danielle's own creative note, and the strongest one: long, deliberate nails holding the can. Ownable, and it puts product and person in the same frame.
- **Brief for Sixty60 crops on the day.** A large share of this trade happens through a phone.

**Agreed approach — fly three in to the braai (J, Aug 2026).** Rather than a second shoot, fly three of the engaged Gauteng creators down and build them into the braai that is already planned. One shoot, one budget, real customers in the hero frames.

- **The distribution is the point, more than the content.** Three creators posting to their own Gauteng followings reaches the geography that is actually buying. The shoot assets are the by-product; the audience access is the asset.
- **Guests, not extras.** Each gets her own setups, not group filler at the edge of frame. If they read as background, the exercise defeats itself.
- **Keep the Cape in frame.** The Cape and international look is doing the aspirational work, and that is why the brand converts. Cape as setting, Gauteng as audience — the buyer is not buying her own suburb. Shoot the mountain, the water, the wine-farm light.
- **Cost is small against a second shoot.** Three flights, a night's accommodation and a fee lands well under a separate production, and it seeds the "1,000 Top Fans" file at the same time.
- **Ask for the posting commitment up front.** Content rights plus an agreed number of posts, agreed before flights are booked.
