  • Today: about 17 cans per store per week. Base takes that to 25.
  • What has to be true: Sixty60 clicks alone give around 600 packs a month, so most of the lift must come from people who see the ads and buy in store.
  • How we'll know: sales in the 15 targeted towns against stores outside them, month on month.

Brand ROAS is our revenue (R124 a pack ex VAT) ÷ spend. ROI uses gross profit of R29.71 a pack in Year 1 and R42.16 in Year 2. Price, margins, store count and baseline are from the brand pro forma; the per-store lift is Johannes's target.

Compliance

  • 18-plus targeting; everyone in frame is over 25.
  • “Not for sale to persons under the age of 18. Drink responsibly.” on every image and caption.
  • No driving, competitor products, sport or drinking-for-success messages (Meta and ARA rules).
