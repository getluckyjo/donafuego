Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Doña Fuego and Fuego Tequila are brands of Doña Distillery (Pty) Ltd, Cape Town, South Africa. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our websites, our Instagram and Facebook presence, and the apps we use to run them, and what we do with it. It is written to meet the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Who is responsible

Doña Distillery (Pty) Ltd is the responsible party. Our Information Officer is Danielle Schoeman, reachable at danielle@dona.co.za.

What we collect

  • Dataroom requests. Name, email address, company and the message you send us when you request access to investor material.
  • Social media. When you follow, like, comment on, message or tag our Instagram or Facebook accounts, we see your public profile and what you sent us. We use Meta's tools to read and reply to that activity, to publish posts, and to measure how our posts and adverts perform.
  • Advertising. Meta shows our adverts to people aged 18 and over in South Africa based on interests and location. Meta tells us how adverts performed in aggregate. We do not receive your name from an advert view or click.
  • Competitions and giveaways. If you enter one, we collect the details the entry asks for, such as name, email, age confirmation and delivery address for a prize.
  • Website use. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, including IP address, browser type and pages visited, to keep the site running and secure.

Why we use it

  • To answer you, whether that is a dataroom request, a comment or a message.
  • To run and improve our Instagram and Facebook accounts and our advertising.
  • To run competitions and deliver prizes.
  • To meet legal duties, including the rule that our drinks are not sold or marketed to persons under 18.

We do not sell personal information. We do not send marketing email unless you have asked for it, and every marketing email carries an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

Service providers that act on our instructions and only as far as the job needs: Meta Platforms (Instagram and Facebook), Google (email and document storage), Vercel (website hosting), Shopify (online store), and our courier for prize deliveries. Some of these providers store data outside South Africa under their own safeguards. We share information with authorities only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Dataroom requests for as long as the investment discussion is live and for five years after, to meet company record-keeping duties. Competition entries for six months after the competition closes. Social media activity for as long as it stays on the platform. Server logs for ninety days.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to its use for direct marketing. Email danielle@dona.co.za and we will respond within thirty days. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Deleting your data

To have your personal information removed from our records, email danielle@dona.co.za with the subject line "Delete my data" and the email address or social media handle you used with us. We will confirm the deletion within thirty days. Information held by Meta, Google, Vercel or Shopify under their own accounts with you is deleted through those platforms.

Cookies

This website sets no tracking cookies of its own. Pages on Instagram, Facebook and Shopify set cookies under those platforms' policies.

Age

Our products are alcoholic beverages. Our websites, social media accounts and adverts are intended for persons aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we delete it if we learn we have.
